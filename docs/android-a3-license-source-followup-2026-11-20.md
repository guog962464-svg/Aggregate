### `libv8_libbase.a`: separate archive notice from retained object scope

At the pinned Node source commit, [V8's main LICENSE](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/e63cab040c09d43ceef8cb35508c40044614e906/deps/v8/LICENSE) says its BSD-style grant covers V8 parts other than externally maintained libraries and directs readers to other applicable notices. Its SHA-256 is `6ab33af8774a0f396ee3aeeb761e3229057682d6f9fa7f572e390c2cb3a6e509`, matching the inventory. The separate [fdlibm notice](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/e63cab040c09d43ceef8cb35508c40044614e906/deps/v8/LICENSE.fdlibm), SHA-256 `e7115e18444dae09d17f361ddc365fb1d342640fe500796209c63f7c80dfae10`, permits use, copying, modification, and distribution subject to preserving its notice.

The pinned [V8 `ieee754.cc`](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/e63cab040c09d43ceef8cb35508c40044614e906/deps/v8/src/base/ieee754.cc) explicitly says it is adapted from fdlibm and reproduces the Sun notice in its header. The inventory ties that source object to retained sections in the exact archive/map above. This narrows the known custom-notice source to `ieee754.o`; it does not show that all 39 retained members, generated material, and headers have been exhaustively checked for other terms, nor does it determine whether the BOM component models every archive member or only linked retained material.

`BSD-3-Clause AND LicenseRef-V8-fdlibm-Sun` remains only a conditional modeling hypothesis for a verified mixed retained scope. The Sun text is not an SPDX-listed license identifier, and a document-local reference needs a stable definition and delivered text. Neither this source mapping nor the candidate expression resolves the full archive row.

### Aggregate V8 source: source tree is broader than linked objects

The aggregate V8 source row is the full `deps/v8` source root, not just the objects retained in `libnode.so`. ### `libnode.so`: static contents, dynamic edges, and notice provenance

The pinned [Node LICENSE](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/e63cab040c09d43ceef8cb35508c40044614e906/LICENSE) applies Node's project license to non-external Node parts and includes separate third-party license text. The inventory's static-link record lists 37 archive inputs, 3,608 archive members, and 2,954 retained archive-object pairs. The binary's dynamic closure is separate from its static source contents. The installed r28c `clang_source_info.md` additionally points to LLVM base commit `3b5e7c83a6e226d5bd7ed2e9b67449b64812074c` and Android's `llvm_android` patch revision `e727bfb014bd436f581a66a450c939a6983a1fc3`. The inventory says Android supplies the four platform libraries, but it does not pin their builds or complete symbol/API closure. ## Result and limitations

The follow-up narrows where known notice text attaches: the fdlibm notice to retained `ieee754.o`; multiple V8 archive scopes to separate source groups; `libc++_shared.so` to its own NDK notice; and the bridge to app source with no evidenced grant. At the time of this 2026-11-20 review, it did not resolve any of the four BOM rows, which had 66/70 library license fields. The later exact-artifact recheck below updates the candidate field count without converting it into legal approval.

For this review, the pinned Node notice files for V8 (`LICENSE`, `LICENSE.fdlibm`, `LICENSE.strongtalk`, `LICENSE.v8`, V8 vendored zlib, Abseil, Highway, simdutf, and Inspector Protocol) were fetched from the official Node commit and their SHA-256 values matched the inventory. The official Node root LICENSE hash `5888…` was fetched separately and compared byte-for-byte with the candidate source root `LICENSE`: the candidate is the exact upstream file followed by the 1,195-byte mobile polywasm notice. The test-signed APK `b13c9446eba58647077a4740ca38edfe998e815ce70ba9dbc2b811397bd5036d` was read to verify its packaged notice asset hash `c373…`. Local hashes were checked for the inventory, `node_probe.cpp`, the candidate Node license asset, the NDK LLVM NOTICE asset, the installed NDK r28c `source.properties`, and its LLVM NOTICE. No build, test, device, accessibility run, or legal approval was performed. The unsigned candidate APK/AAB and raw linker map were not re-opened or rebuilt during this source review.
